Powers Search and Research Chat. Installed privately in your own workspace; DAAS does not keep it. How do I get this?

  1. Go to platform.openai.com and sign in (or create an account).
  2. Add a payment method under Billing (OpenAI bills you directly for usage — typically a few dollars a month).
  3. Open API keys in the left menu, click Create new secret key.
  4. Copy the key (it starts with sk-) and paste it above.

Online donations · Stripe — optional

Donations go straight into your own Stripe account. Your secret key is stored privately in your workspace — never in your browser. How do I get these?

  1. Go to dashboard.stripe.com and sign in (or create a free account).
  2. Open Developers → API keys (top-right menu, then the left panel).
  3. Copy the Publishable key and paste it in the first box.
  4. Click Reveal on the Secret key, copy it, and paste it in the second box.
