Daily Five

Every morning, five people to reach out to.

Open DAAS and your list is waiting: five names, and the reason each one is on it. Call, text or stop by, then tap once to log how it went. Tomorrow’s list already knows.

  • Follow-upYou tried and didn’t connect. DAAS brings them back on a schedule until you do.
  • ReconnectIt has been longer than usual since you spoke. DAAS learns each family’s own rhythm.
  • NewJoined your community recently and hasn’t had a personal hello yet.
  • ProspectSomeone in your area you haven’t gotten to know.

A connection list, not a fundraising list: no giving requirement, and no money in the ordering.

Every interaction, remembered

Calls, texts, visits, meetings, classes, events, donations and notes all land on one timeline per household. Open anyone and you see when you last spoke, what was said, who came to what, and whether the relationship is Warm, Steady or Cool.

Know a family well but never wrote it down? Tell DAAS everything you know, in your own words, and it sorts it into their profile.

Quick Capture

Talk for thirty seconds. DAAS does the data entry.

Leaving a visit? Record a voice note from the car. Forward an email chain, snap the note on your desk, or screenshot a conversation. DAAS works out who it is about, what happened and what should happen next, then shows you exactly what it wants to save.

Nothing sends or saves until you approve it.

How AI works in DAAS

AI where it’s needed. Only there.

Choosing who you should call doesn’t use AI at all. DAAS looks at each family’s history, when you last spoke, how often you usually do, who is new, and brings the right names forward. AI steps in only for the busywork: turning voice notes into records, drafting emails and answering questions.

No AI

Choosing who to call

Daily Five, follow-ups and Prospect Finder work from your own records: calls, visits, events and gifts. It costs nothing to run, and every name comes with the reason it is there.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your CRM lives in your own Supabase account, an online database you create and control, and you can open it whenever you like. DAAS gives you simple screens for everyday work, but DAAS cannot access or read your data.

Who pays for the AI?

You do, directly, and only for what you use. You add your own OpenAI key, the technology behind ChatGPT, and OpenAI bills you. It powers Quick Capture, Research Chat, Profile Builder and the DAAS Assistant. Choosing who to call doesn’t use AI, so that part costs nothing.

What about email?

Email is included. DAAS sends through Amazon SES, and we help you set up your own domain, so every message comes from your own address, with opens and clicks tracked for your community.

Can DAAS process donations and handle events?

Yes. Build donation, registration and event forms and put them on your website. Every signup goes straight into DAAS, and donations run through your own Stripe account, so each gift is recorded with the family who gave it.

How do I get started?

Request DAAS below and we will send you an access code. The setup wizard builds your private CRM in a few minutes, then you sign in. Your AI and payment keys can be added then or later.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open DAAS on your phone and add it to your home screen. Your Daily Five, voice notes, logging and the map all work one-handed on the go.
