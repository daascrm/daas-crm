The CRM that tells you what to do next

A complete CRM — that doesn't stop at remembering.

DAAS keeps your contacts, families, donations, events, communications, notes, tasks, and the full history of every relationship in one place. But where an ordinary CRM stops at storing it all, DAAS helps you act on it — turning everything you've collected into a clear answer to the only question that matters on a busy morning: who needs you today?

The Daily Five

Start every morning knowing exactly who to call.

Every day begins with the same question — who should I reach out to today? DAAS answers it with five people drawn from across your community: someone waiting on a follow-up, someone you've quietly lost touch with, a family you just met, a longtime supporter who's overdue for a call, and someone with real potential you've never gotten to know. Each name comes with a plain reason — and when you've made your calls, you're done.

Prospect Finder

Find the opportunities hiding in your community.

When your community runs to hundreds of households, the people most worth investing in are the easiest to lose in the crowd. Prospect Finder brings them forward — the business owner you've only met once, the family that comes to everything but has never become supporters, the longtime donor you haven't spoken to in months. The opportunities were always there; DAAS just makes them easy to see.

One relationship history

Every conversation starts with the whole story.

DAAS remembers everything behind a household — the visits and conversations, the emails and texts, the classes they attend, their children's programs, what they've given, and the personal notes you'd never want to lose. It all comes together into one timeline, alongside a read on the relationship and how best to approach it, so you never walk into a conversation cold again.

Quick Capture

Get information in without hours of typing.

The most valuable details never make it into a CRM, because entering them takes time you don't have. So don't type them. Paste an email, jot a quick thought, photograph a handwritten note, forward a screenshot — DAAS reads it, figures out which household it belongs to, and proposes what to log. Nothing saves until you confirm.

AI, where it helps

Say what you need in plain English.

Ask DAAS to send an email to a group, spin up an event or a form, or pull a list of people — describe it plainly and the right tool opens already filled in, with nothing sent until you say so. You can ask questions across your whole community too. The Daily Five and the relationship engine, though, run on clear, consistent logic — no black box, no cost per question — so every recommendation comes with a reason you can see for yourself.

FAQ

The honest answers.

Is DAAS a fundraising CRM?

It's a relationship CRM with fundraising built in. Healthy fundraising grows out of healthy relationships, so donor management is part of the system — but the point is helping you know your community and care for people more consistently, not chasing gifts.

The morning list proves it: it ranks by who's been waiting longest, not by net worth.

Do I need AI to use DAAS?

No. The Daily Five and the relationship engine work without it — plain, explainable logic, no key and no per-question cost. Every card can tell you exactly why it chose someone.

AI is there for the moments it truly helps: searching your history, drafting an email, building an event, reading a handwritten note. It's an upgrade, not the main feature — we recommend adding an API key to unlock the upgraded features.

What happens to all my existing contacts?

Bring them with you. DAAS imports your existing contacts and history, so you can finally run everything from one relationship CRM instead of juggling a database here and a spreadsheet there.

What does "DAAS" mean?

דעת — da'as: knowing. In Chassidus it's the faculty of connection — the knowing that binds you to someone, not just knowledge about them. For a CRM built around relationships, the name is the whole idea.
